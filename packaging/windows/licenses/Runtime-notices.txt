EasyRider loads Qt 6.9.3 Core, Gui and Widgets and their Windows plugins as
separate, replaceable DLLs under LGPL version 3. The installed licenses
directory includes the LGPL, GPL, the Qt Base SPDX bill of materials (including
third-party notices), and the MinGW runtime notices and GCC runtime exception.
Users may replace the libraries and reverse engineer the application for
debugging modifications to those libraries.

The EasyRider-win64 download includes qtbase-everywhere-src-6.9.3.tar.xz
alongside the installer. Keep both files available when redistributing.

Upstream source:
Qt Base 6.9.3: https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.9/6.9.3/submodules/qtbase-everywhere-src-6.9.3.tar.xz
GCC 13.1.0: https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/gcc/gcc-13.1.0/
MinGW-w64 (including winpthreads): https://www.mingw-w64.org/downloads/
